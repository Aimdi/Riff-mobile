# Riff redesign v2: "as good as the best"

The 1.7 restyle ("X Lights out, Riff green") was visual only: no element
could move. v2 is the follow-up the owner asked for: bring Home, the
library (songs, playlists, albums, artists), Podcasts and Audiobooks up to
the level of the best music and podcast apps, layouts included.

## What stays

- **The side rail**: same width (47 dp), items, order and behaviour.
- **Theme rules**: background #000000; the accent the person picked (green
  by default; Blue, Violet, Crimson, Amber, Cyan keep working) only where
  RIFF_UI_RESTYLE.md §2.5 allows; colours, text and spacing from ThemeData,
  RiffColors and RiffSpacing; album colour only tints the full player.
- **Behaviour**: playback, queue, downloads, podcasts, ad-skip, SponsorBlock,
  sync, Android Auto, settings persistence. A redesign moves and restyles
  things; it never changes what a control does.
- **Motion**: RiffDurations / RiffMotionDistances and the shared kit in
  `riff_motion.dart`; Remove animations honoured everywhere.

## References

What each app does best, and what Riff takes from it.

| App | Strength | Riff takes |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | Home opens on a 2-column grid of recents; shelves of big square art; cards shrink under the finger; Your Library is one list with type chips, sort and a grid/list switch, pins on top | Jump back in grid; press feedback; library chips/sort/pins |
| YouTube Music | Speed dial; Quick picks; mood chips; "Listen again"; library chips | Speed dial, Quick picks, station chips (Discover) |
| Apple Music (iOS) | Large bold page titles; big "Top picks" cards; Recently played grid; library as a short list of categories, then big-art grids; pinned items | Large titles; category list at the top of the library tabs |
| Stock OS players (Samsung Music, Pixel/Google, iOS Music) | Plain tab sets (Tracks / Albums / Artists / Playlists / Folders); A–Z fast scroller; shuffle-all row on top of Tracks; album grid with year | Shuffle row; A–Z scrubber on long lists; album year under the title |
| Pocket Casts | Podcast grid with unplayed badges; Up Next as a first-class place; filters (New releases, In progress, Downloaded); show pages with tinted header and big Follow button | Grid badges; filters as chips; show header |
| Apple Podcasts | "Up Next" shelf of large episode cards with progress; show pages with a big cover and Latest episode | Continue cards with a progress ring |
| Audible | Library with chips (All, Not started, In progress, Finished, Downloaded); a big "Continue listening" card; progress under every cover | Audiobooks chips; progress bars on covers; hero continue card |

## Principles

1. **Art first.** Covers carry the page: bigger, consistent radii
   (RiffSizes / RiffRadii), titles under them, nothing over them except a
   play button or a progress bar.
2. **One look per kind of thing.** A playlist card on Home, in the library
   grid and on an artist page is the same card. A song row is the same row
   everywhere.
3. **Clear page titles.** Each rail page starts with its name large
   (headlineSmall), the way Apple Music and Spotify's Library do; Home keeps
   the greeting.
4. **Feedback on touch.** Every card and tile shrinks a touch under the
   finger (RiffPressScale), rows keep their ink ripple.
5. **Calm motion.** Content fades in where its skeleton was, pages fade
   through, lists don't jump when data arrives.
6. **Phones first, tablets too.** Widths come from the pane (screen minus
   rail); grids add columns as the pane widens; small phones (360 dp) keep
   two columns.

## Home (first pass)

From the baseline renders: Speed dial was nine bare covers (no way to tell
what a tile plays), every shelf was the same row of square cards, and
hairlines plus 32 dp gaps split the feed into boxes.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Speed dial tiles carry their title on a dark wash at the bottom (centred on artist circles; 12 sp on small tiles) | YouTube Music Speed dial | A tile says what it plays |
| Shelves of songs (Listen again, Because you played…) are columns of four song rows, paged like Quick picks, with Play all | YouTube Music Quick picks / Listen again | Songs read as songs; the feed varies instead of repeating one card |
| No hairlines between sections; 16 dp above a section's 48 dp title row, 4 dp below it | Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music feeds | Rhythm from space alone; ~28 dp to a title, 16 dp to its covers |
| Card titles 14 medium (were 15 bold) | Spotify / YouTube Music / Apple Music card text | Lighter than the section title above |
| Jump back in titles 12 sp on narrow tiles | — | Fewer words split across lines on 360 dp phones |
| Cards shrink a touch under the finger | Spotify, Apple Music | Touch feedback on art |

Kept: the section set and order (`buildHomeSections`), the greeting row,
Jump back in, Quick picks' hero carousel (Apple Music's Top picks), the
side rail.

Also fixed from the renders: on tablets 800–1000 dp wide the mini
player's right-hand buttons spilled out of the bar (23 dp); they now show
as many as fit, most important first.

## Home: shelf headers that say why

YouTube Music sends more than a title for a shelf about someone: a line
over it ("Similar to"; your name over Listen again) and a picture (the
artist's photo, round; yours). Riff dropped both, so a real "Similar to"
shelf showed a bare artist name, sentence-cased ("Nova hart"), and was
filed with the plain editorial shelves.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The parser keeps the header's line and picture (`parseShelfHeader` → `ShelfHeader`, cached with the shelf) | YouTube Music | The shelf says why it's there |
| A 40 dp picture before the header: round for an artist or account, a cover with 4 dp corners for a song | YouTube Music, Spotify "More like" | Recognised at a glance |
| A name under its line keeps its capitals (and may take a second line on a phone rather than lose its end); the line is read with the title when sorting shelves, so "Similar to" + a name moves up with the personal shelves | YouTube Music | "Nova Hart", in the right place |
| "Because you liked" over the song's title, with its cover, instead of the whole phrase as a title cut short | Spotify, YouTube Music | The song is the point, and it fits |
| "Spotify" over the first Spotify shelf only | Spotify's own Home | Said once is enough |
| The name and its picture open its page (the artist, an album), as YouTube Music links them; any other title opens See all, as Spotify's and Apple Music's do. For TalkBack the title is a button only when it opens something of its own (See all keeps the chevron) | YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music | The whole header works, not just the chevron |

TalkBack reads one heading ("Similar to, Nova Hart"); the picture is
decorative. Section set and order unchanged.

## Home: See all

A shelf of covers showed two and a half at a time; a long one could only
be swiped through. Spotify's Show all, YouTube Music's More and Apple
Music's See All open the section whole.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A chevron in the header of a card shelf that runs past a pane and a half (`ShelfCardSize.hasMore`) | Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music | Everything at a glance |
| The page: the shelf's title (its line under it), every card in a grid, two columns on a phone and more on a tablet; videos and episodes one per row on a phone (`HomeShelfScreen`) | Spotify Show all | The same cards, so tap, long press and the cover flight work as on Home |

Song-row shelves (Listen again, Because you liked) keep their Play all.

## Home: New episodes

Home showed the shows you follow (covers) and the episode you're in the
middle of, but not what's come out: you had to open Podcasts › Inbox.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A New episodes shelf before Your shows: the Inbox's unplayed episodes, newest first, at most 12 (`PodcastInboxCounts.waiting`), from this session's Inbox or the one the phone kept | Spotify Home's New episodes, Pocket Casts' New Releases | What's new, where you start |
| Episode cards: title, show, then "Today · 58m" (or "12m left") as the Inbox's rows; long press opens the Inbox's episode menu; a YouTube episode goes to WizeStream when that's chosen, as from the Inbox | Spotify, Pocket Casts episode cards | The same episode, the same actions |

An episode you've started is in Jump back in, so the shelf leaves it out.

## Home: YouTube Music's podcast shelves

YouTube Music's Home has podcast shelves (shows, episodes) and audiobooks.
Riff's parser knew only albums, artists, playlists and songs: podcast and
audiobook cards came out empty, and a shelf was judged by its first card,
so one led by a card Home doesn't show (a profile) was dropped whole.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A podcast's card opens its page (a playlist of kind `podcast`, as search has them); an audiobook's opens as an album | YouTube Music Home | The shelf as YouTube Music sends it |
| Episode cards (`musicMultiRowListItemRenderer`) are episodes, shown on episode cards, not song rows | YouTube Music Home | An episode reads as one |
| A card of a kind Home doesn't show is left out instead of becoming an empty first card | — | The rest of its shelf stays |

## Home on tablets

Speed dial's tiles stopped at 160 dp, so on a tablet its three columns
left a strip empty at the right while Jump back in and Quick picks filled
the pane. They now grow to 200 dp (`RiffSizes.speedTileMax`): three
columns fill a tablet's pane; phones are unchanged (about 105 dp).

## Home: Recently played

Home had the episode or book to resume (Jump back in) and recent songs
(Speed dial), but not the album or playlist you were listening to: that
took Library › sort by Recently played.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A Recently played shelf, first among Riff's own: the albums and playlists you last played from, latest first, at most 12 (`recentCollectionsShelf`) | Spotify Recently played, Apple Music Recently Played, YouTube Music Listen again | Back to what you were playing in a tap |
| `CollectionRecents` keeps a collection's name, cover and who it's by with the time it already kept (`collectionPlayedInfo`); your Library's lists and saved playlists and albums are read from the Library, so ones played before show at once | — | The shelf needs no network |

The Recently played list itself (songs) is left out; nothing is kept in a
private session.

## Library (first pass)

from Harmony Music's old top search bar), the Songs bar packed the count,
sort, three unlabeled filter icons, search and ⋮ into one row (the count
cut to "22 s…", the row spilling over on 360 dp phones), the built-in
lists were grey squares, and artists were two huge circles per row.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Every rail tab (Library, Discover, Podcasts, Audiobooks, Settings) starts just under the status bar, where Home's greeting is | Spotify, Apple Music page titles | ~60 dp more content; the same top on every tab |
| Page titles in the greeting's style (24 sp heavy, 48 dp row) | Apple Music, Spotify Your Library | One title style for every page |
| Songs: Downloaded / Cloud / On this phone as a row of labelled chips above the bar | Spotify Your Library chips | Labelled filters; the bar keeps count, sort, search, ⋮ and fits on small phones |
| Favorites, Recently played, Cached, Downloads get a generated colour cover with their glyph in white | Spotify's Liked Songs cover | Built-in lists look like the rest of the grid |
| Pinned Favorites / Recently played tiles lose the play glyph (the tile opens; it was never a button) | — | Honest affordance; titles no longer cut on small phones |
| Artists: three columns of circles on phones, name only | Spotify's artist grid | More artists per screen; "Artist" under each was redundant on the Artists tab |
| Artist cards shrink under the finger | Spotify, Apple Music | Same feedback as every card |

## Audiobooks

Continue listening opens on the latest book, large: cover, title, author,
progress bar with the time left (the chapter for a free book) and an
accent play button, as Audible opens the library. The other books in
progress stay on the row under it.

On phones the play button sits on the cover, as on the continue cards of
Home and Podcasts, so the title, author and chapter get the card's width
(the chapter line was cut to "Chapter 7 o…" on a small phone). From
480 dp up it keeps its own place at the end. A free book's genre chips
hug their labels (each one was as wide as the page).

A book on your Audiobookshelf server now has the free book's page (one
look per kind of thing): the cover centred and square (it was a 2:3
crop beside the title, while the library shows the same cover square),
title, author, narrator, then "14 chapters · 16h 10m", and the wide
pill says where Play picks up ("Resume · Chapter 7 of 14"; it said
Continue listening only when the server knew of a place, not this
phone). The description folds to four lines under More (on both pages
More now shows only when there is more to read), and the chapters are
the free book's rows: number, title, length, the playing
one in the accent with moving bars, a thin bar under one partly heard.
The row's separate play glyph went (the whole row plays it). On a
tablet the pill stops at 400 dp instead of crossing the page. Both pages
share `audiobook_book_page.dart`.

## Podcasts

Episode rows (Inbox, Queue, Downloads, search) date episodes as Pocket
Casts and Apple Podcasts do: Today, Yesterday, "22 Oct" this year, the
full date before. When the meta line runs out of room the show name is
cut short, not the date and length. Downloads use the same row as Inbox
and Queue (progress, time left, one hairline). The Subscriptions grid
counts columns from the pane beside the rail: 3 on phones, 2 on small
phones.

## Long lists

Full song lists, albums and playlists get a fast-scroll thumb on the
right edge once they are three screens long (Spotify, the phone's own
music players). Dragging it moves through the whole list; when the list
is in title or artist order a bubble shows the letter.

## Artist page

Already a single Spotify-style scroll (photo header, Follow and play,
Popular, shelves, Fans also like). About is now a card, as Spotify has
it: the artist's photo (16:9, at most 260 dp tall), the views count,
then the bio. The header photo fades out with a mask instead of a layer
of page colour over it: a header whose height fell between two pixels
(small phones) showed the photo's last row as a line under the name.
The harness renders the page (`artist`, `artist_full`).

## Settings

The groups (Appearance, Listening, ...) had titles a size up from list
titles anywhere else (titleLarge); they use titleMedium now, and the
line under each may wrap to a second line instead of stopping at
"Theme, language, playe..." on a small phone. The harness renders
Settings (`settings`, `settings_full`); every other screen rendered
with no overflow on phone, small phone and tablet.

## Search

The top result sits on a surface card of its own (YouTube Music, Spotify);
the row inside and what a tap does are unchanged. Search starts under the
status bar like every tab.

## Album and playlist pages

On a tablet the cover behind the header ran under the whole track list
(it was as tall as the screen is wide); it is now at most 440 dp tall,
cropped to fill. Phones unchanged.

The cover flies from the card you tapped to the page's cover and back
(Apple Music, Spotify): from a Home shelf, the library's albums and
playlists, an artist's releases and playlists, and search results. The nested navigator
has a Hero controller now (Material's arc path), each card's cover is
tagged with the card itself, so the same album on two shelves never
clashes, and only the card that was tapped hands its tag on
(`CoverHero`). When the cover flies in, the page's title and buttons
fade in beside it instead of the whole header; a page opened any other
way (a link, the player, Android Auto) fades in as before. A card that
plays on tap (the "play collections on tap" setting) flies nothing.
Shows and books do the same: a show's cover flies from the Subscriptions
grid, a folder, the Podcasts shelves or Home to its page, and a book's
from the Audiobooks shelves to the free or store book page. These pages
open over the whole app, and a flight starts from the tab underneath
just as well. The Audiobookshelf book page draws its cover only once the
book has loaded, so nothing flies there; showing it sooner would change
the page. The harness renders `album_from_home`, `album_from_library`,
`album_from_artist`, `podcast_show_from_subs` and
`audiobook_from_discover` through the flight, and checks a cover is in
the air halfway.

## QA sweep (every screen, four sizes)

All 59 harness screens rendered on phone, small phone, tablet and large
text, with no errors. What was wrong and is fixed:

- A page header's line under the title takes up to three lines (was
  two), so Never play's "… You can still play them yourself." and the
  Sync pages' lines aren't cut short on a small phone or at large text.
- Empty states keep their message to 360 dp on a tablet (a few short
  lines, not one line across the screen); phones are narrower anyway.
- Home's load error says "Couldn't load Home. Check your connection."
  with a plain "Retry" (was "Oops network error!" and "Retry!"); English
  only, other languages keep their words.
- Episode rows (Inbox, Queue, show pages): when the date and length can't
  keep their share of the line beside the show (large text on a phone),
  the show takes a line of its own above them, instead of "Th… · Today ·
  …".
- Library › Songs: Favorites and Recently played read whole ("Recently /
  played" on two lines, was "Recently pla…"), both tiles one height; at
  large text a word too wide for the tile is set a little smaller rather
  than broken in two ("Favorite / s").
- The playlist dialog's title takes two lines at large text ("Create new
  playlist", was "Create new pla…").
- Lyrics fade out to nothing at the top and bottom of their card
  (Spotify, Apple Music); a line the edge cut stayed half there on a small
  phone and at large text. The darker edges of the card are kept.

Looked at and left: the Plugins and settings pages run the full width on
a tablet (as Android's own settings do); long titles in the player and
page headers end in "…" (one line, as Spotify's do).

## Stats

The harness now renders Stats, Rewind and the podcast stats. Stats'
tiles were a fixed shape (1.9:1) and spilled on a small phone and at
large text; now each pair of tiles is as tall as its taller one, and a
label may take two lines. The week's chart is labelled by weekday ("M T
W…", today's letter brighter) instead of "10-16", and TalkBack reads each
bar with its date and plays ("Tuesday, October 21, 2026: 26 Plays"). Top songs carry their covers, as
Spotify's top tracks do, and the artists tile says Artists (it counted
the artists heard, under the title "Top artists"). Rewind's three cards
grow with their labels instead of cutting "songs played" at large text,
and its most-played song shows its cover.

## Settings pages

The harness renders Home layout and Podcast settings too. Home layout
cut its longer section names to one line ("Mixes, your shows and new
releas…"); a row now takes a second line for them. The podcast stats
card's top shows keep a gap between a long title and its time.

Never play, Sync, gpodder sync, Recognize and Plugins render too. Page
subtitles under a title (Never play's "Kept out of mixes, Riff Wave,
radio, autoplay and recommendations…", Sync's, Podcast settings') were
cut to one line; they take two now. Never play, when empty, shows the
same empty state as Podcasts and Home offline (a glyph over the
message) instead of a line of grey text.

## Sheets

The harness renders a song's ⋮ sheet, Add to playlist and the sleep
timer. Add to playlist listed every playlist behind the same list glyph;
each now shows its cover, as Spotify's does (a Piped playlist keeps its
cloud).

## Large text

The harness also renders every screen on a small phone at the largest
font size Riff follows (1.3x, `large`): nothing spills. The tab titles
(Podcasts, Library, Audiobooks, Settings) now shrink to fit their row
there, as Home's greeting does, instead of being cut to "Pod…" by the
header's buttons.

## Accessibility

The harness can check Flutter's accessibility guidelines on every screen
(`SHOT_A11Y=true`). The mini player failed them on all of them: its
controls were 24 to 28 dp tall to tap, and TalkBack met an unnamed bar,
an unnamed song area and the cover, title and artist as separate nodes.
Now each control takes the bar's full height on phones (the glyphs are
unchanged), the song reads as one button ("title, artist") that opens
the player, with Go to album and View artist as its actions, and the bar
is named Now playing. Home's sections, whose long press opens Hide
section / Home layout, carry their names too.

The same check then named the rest of what TalkBack met without a name:
the episode play pills (Inbox, Queue, a show's page), the search
suggestion's fill-in arrow, the top-result artist row, and every card
that springs on press (the press effect added a second, unnamed tap).
The album and playlist cover's tap, a shortcut for Play, is left to the
Play button. In the full player the cover reads "Cover art" (its tap
shows the lyrics), the Up Next bar reads "Up Next · next song", and the
play button's hold-to-seek stays a finger gesture (TalkBack has the seek
slider). The new-playlist button is named. All 34 phone screens in the
harness now pass the labelled tap targets guideline.

Song rows' like and ⋮ buttons get the same treatment: still 24 dp wide
side by side, but 48 dp tall to tap (they were 24 by 24).

The small play badges on Home's cards and the podcast covers keep their
look, but the corner they sit in is now a 48 dp square to tap
(`CoverTapTarget`), named as Play for TalkBack; the rest of the cover
still opens the page.

Contrast: the full player's artist line and times were the grey
secondary text, under 3:1 over a cover-coloured page. They are now the
text colour at 70%, as Spotify and Apple Music write their second line,
and so is "Up Next" on the bar under the controls. The player passes the
text contrast guideline. (Elsewhere the grey secondary text on black is
about 4.46:1, a hair under 4.5; the check's lower readings there are thin
glyphs measured against black.)

The newer screens in the harness (book pages, Stats, sheets, settings
pages) went through the same check. Switches beside a title (Home
layout's sections, the sleep timer's Shake to restart and Turn on
automatically, Podcast settings, a show's new-episode alerts) were
unnamed toggles; each row is now one node, read as "Speed dial, switch,
on", as ListTile rows already were.

Chips and section pills (Stats' ranges, the podcast speed and skip
choices, Podcasts' Inbox / Queue / Subscriptions, Audiobooks' sections,
Search's result filters) didn't say which one was chosen; TalkBack now
hears "All time, selected", as Material's chips say. They stay 32 dp
tall: a 48 dp target would add height to every row of chips.

Left as they are: the rail's unselected labels are a little under 4.5:1
(the rail stays as it is), and the five mini player controls stay under
48 dp wide (the title would have no room).
