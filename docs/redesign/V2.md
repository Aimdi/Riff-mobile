# Riff redesign v2: "as good as the best"

The 1.7 restyle ("X Lights out, Riff green") was visual only: no element
could move. v2 is the follow-up the owner asked for: bring Home, the
library (songs, playlists, albums, artists), Podcasts and Audiobooks up to
the level of the best music and podcast apps, layouts included.

## What stays

- **The side rail**: same width (47 dp), items, order and behaviour.
- **Theme rules**: background #000000; the accent the person picked (green
  by default; Blue, Violet, Crimson, Amber, Cyan keep working) only where
  RIFF_UI_RESTYLE.md §2.5 allows; colours, text and spacing from ThemeData,
  RiffColors and RiffSpacing; album colour only tints the full player.
- **Behaviour**: playback, queue, downloads, podcasts, ad-skip, SponsorBlock,
  sync, Android Auto, settings persistence. A redesign moves and restyles
  things; it never changes what a control does.
- **Motion**: RiffDurations / RiffMotionDistances and the shared kit in
  `riff_motion.dart`; Remove animations honoured everywhere.

## References

What each app does best, and what Riff takes from it.

| App | Strength | Riff takes |
|---|---|---|
| Spotify | Home opens on a 2-column grid of recents; shelves of big square art; cards shrink under the finger; Your Library is one list with type chips, sort and a grid/list switch, pins on top | Jump back in grid; press feedback; library chips/sort/pins |
| YouTube Music | Speed dial; Quick picks; mood chips; "Listen again"; library chips | Speed dial, Quick picks, station chips (Discover) |
| Apple Music (iOS) | Large bold page titles; big "Top picks" cards; Recently played grid; library as a short list of categories, then big-art grids; pinned items | Large titles; category list at the top of the library tabs |
| Stock OS players (Samsung Music, Pixel/Google, iOS Music) | Plain tab sets (Tracks / Albums / Artists / Playlists / Folders); A–Z fast scroller; shuffle-all row on top of Tracks; album grid with year | Shuffle row; A–Z scrubber on long lists; album year under the title |
| Pocket Casts | Podcast grid with unplayed badges; Up Next as a first-class place; filters (New releases, In progress, Downloaded); show pages with tinted header and big Follow button | Grid badges; filters as chips; show header |
| Apple Podcasts | "Up Next" shelf of large episode cards with progress; show pages with a big cover and Latest episode | Continue cards with a progress ring |
| Audible | Library with chips (All, Not started, In progress, Finished, Downloaded); a big "Continue listening" card; progress under every cover | Audiobooks chips; progress bars on covers; hero continue card |

## Principles

1. **Art first.** Covers carry the page: bigger, consistent radii
   (RiffSizes / RiffRadii), titles under them, nothing over them except a
   play button or a progress bar.
2. **One look per kind of thing.** A playlist card on Home, in the library
   grid and on an artist page is the same card. A song row is the same row
   everywhere.
3. **Clear page titles.** Each rail page starts with its name large
   (headlineSmall), the way Apple Music and Spotify's Library do; Home keeps
   the greeting.
4. **Feedback on touch.** Every card and tile shrinks a touch under the
   finger (RiffPressScale), rows keep their ink ripple.
5. **Calm motion.** Content fades in where its skeleton was, pages fade
   through, lists don't jump when data arrives.
6. **Phones first, tablets too.** Widths come from the pane (screen minus
   rail); grids add columns as the pane widens; small phones (360 dp) keep
   two columns.

## Home (first pass)

From the baseline renders: Speed dial was nine bare covers (no way to tell
what a tile plays), every shelf was the same row of square cards, and
hairlines plus 32 dp gaps split the feed into boxes.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Speed dial tiles carry their title on a dark wash at the bottom (centred on artist circles; 12 sp on small tiles) | YouTube Music Speed dial | A tile says what it plays |
| Shelves of songs (Listen again, Because you played…) are columns of four song rows, paged like Quick picks, with Play all | YouTube Music Quick picks / Listen again | Songs read as songs; the feed varies instead of repeating one card |
| No hairlines between sections; 16 dp above a section's 48 dp title row, 4 dp below it | Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music feeds | Rhythm from space alone; ~28 dp to a title, 16 dp to its covers |
| Card titles 14 medium (were 15 bold) | Spotify / YouTube Music / Apple Music card text | Lighter than the section title above |
| Jump back in titles 12 sp on narrow tiles | — | Fewer words split across lines on 360 dp phones |
| Cards shrink a touch under the finger | Spotify, Apple Music | Touch feedback on art |

Kept: the section set and order (`buildHomeSections`), the greeting row,
Jump back in, Quick picks' hero carousel (Apple Music's Top picks), the
side rail.

Also fixed from the renders: on tablets 800–1000 dp wide the mini
player's right-hand buttons spilled out of the bar (23 dp); they now show
as many as fit, most important first.

## Library (first pass)

From the baseline renders: every tab began 90 dp down (a fixed gap left
from Harmony Music's old top search bar), the Songs bar packed the count,
sort, three unlabeled filter icons, search and ⋮ into one row (the count
cut to "22 s…", the row spilling over on 360 dp phones), the built-in
lists were grey squares, and artists were two huge circles per row.

| Change | From | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Every rail tab (Library, Discover, Podcasts, Audiobooks, Settings) starts just under the status bar, where Home's greeting is | Spotify, Apple Music page titles | ~60 dp more content; the same top on every tab |
| Page titles in the greeting's style (24 sp heavy, 48 dp row) | Apple Music, Spotify Your Library | One title style for every page |
| Songs: Downloaded / Cloud / On this phone as a row of labelled chips above the bar | Spotify Your Library chips | Labelled filters; the bar keeps count, sort, search, ⋮ and fits on small phones |
| Favorites, Recently played, Cached, Downloads get a generated colour cover with their glyph in white | Spotify's Liked Songs cover | Built-in lists look like the rest of the grid |
| Pinned Favorites / Recently played tiles lose the play glyph (the tile opens; it was never a button) | — | Honest affordance; titles no longer cut on small phones |
| Artists: three columns of circles on phones, name only | Spotify's artist grid | More artists per screen; "Artist" under each was redundant on the Artists tab |
| Artist cards shrink under the finger | Spotify, Apple Music | Same feedback as every card |

## Audiobooks

Continue listening opens on the latest book, large: cover, title, author,
progress bar with the time left (the chapter for a free book) and an
accent play button, as Audible opens the library. The other books in
progress stay on the row under it.

On phones the play button sits on the cover, as on the continue cards of
Home and Podcasts, so the title, author and chapter get the card's width
(the chapter line was cut to "Chapter 7 o…" on a small phone). From
480 dp up it keeps its own place at the end. A free book's genre chips
hug their labels (each one was as wide as the page).

## Podcasts

Episode rows (Inbox, Queue, Downloads, search) date episodes as Pocket
Casts and Apple Podcasts do: Today, Yesterday, "22 Oct" this year, the
full date before. When the meta line runs out of room the show name is
cut short, not the date and length. Downloads use the same row as Inbox
and Queue (progress, time left, one hairline). The Subscriptions grid
counts columns from the pane beside the rail: 3 on phones, 2 on small
phones.

## Long lists

Full song lists, albums and playlists get a fast-scroll thumb on the
right edge once they are three screens long (Spotify, the phone's own
music players). Dragging it moves through the whole list; when the list
is in title or artist order a bubble shows the letter.

## Artist page

Already a single Spotify-style scroll (photo header, Follow and play,
Popular, shelves, Fans also like). About is now a card, as Spotify has
it: the artist's photo (16:9, at most 260 dp tall), the views count,
then the bio. The header photo fades out with a mask instead of a layer
of page colour over it: a header whose height fell between two pixels
(small phones) showed the photo's last row as a line under the name.
The harness renders the page (`artist`, `artist_full`).

## Settings

The groups (Appearance, Listening, ...) had titles a size up from list
titles anywhere else (titleLarge); they use titleMedium now, and the
line under each may wrap to a second line instead of stopping at
"Theme, language, playe..." on a small phone. The harness renders
Settings (`settings`, `settings_full`); every other screen rendered
with no overflow on phone, small phone and tablet.

## Search

The top result sits on a surface card of its own (YouTube Music, Spotify);
the row inside and what a tap does are unchanged. Search starts under the
status bar like every tab.

## Album and playlist pages

On a tablet the cover behind the header ran under the whole track list
(it was as tall as the screen is wide); it is now at most 440 dp tall,
cropped to fill. Phones unchanged.

Considered and left for later: a shared-element (Hero) move from a card
to the page's cover. The pages live in a nested navigator without a Hero
controller, and the header fades in over the cover, so the move would
need both changed.

## Large text

The harness also renders every screen on a small phone at the largest
font size Riff follows (1.3x, `large`): nothing spills. The tab titles
(Podcasts, Library, Audiobooks, Settings) now shrink to fit their row
there, as Home's greeting does, instead of being cut to "Pod…" by the
header's buttons.

## Accessibility

The harness can check Flutter's accessibility guidelines on every screen
(`SHOT_A11Y=true`). The mini player failed them on all of them: its
controls were 24 to 28 dp tall to tap, and TalkBack met an unnamed bar,
an unnamed song area and the cover, title and artist as separate nodes.
Now each control takes the bar's full height on phones (the glyphs are
unchanged), the song reads as one button ("title, artist") that opens
the player, with Go to album and View artist as its actions, and the bar
is named Now playing. Home's sections, whose long press opens Hide
section / Home layout, carry their names too.

The same check then named the rest of what TalkBack met without a name:
the episode play pills (Inbox, Queue, a show's page), the search
suggestion's fill-in arrow, the top-result artist row, and every card
that springs on press (the press effect added a second, unnamed tap).
The album and playlist cover's tap, a shortcut for Play, is left to the
Play button. In the full player the cover reads "Cover art" (its tap
shows the lyrics), the Up Next bar reads "Up Next · next song", and the
play button's hold-to-seek stays a finger gesture (TalkBack has the seek
slider). The new-playlist button is named. All 34 phone screens in the
harness now pass the labelled tap targets guideline.

Song rows' like and ⋮ buttons get the same treatment: still 24 dp wide
side by side, but 48 dp tall to tap (they were 24 by 24).

Left as they are: the rail's unselected labels are a little under 4.5:1
(the rail stays as it is), and the five mini player controls stay under
48 dp wide (the title would have no room).
